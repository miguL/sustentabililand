4. Privacy policy

Data controller. Sustentabililand, Lda, at the contact details above.

What we collect. Only what you send us through the contact form, by email, by phone or on WhatsApp: name, phone number, email, town and the content of your message.

What we use it for. To reply to your enquiry or quote request and, if you go ahead with us, to provide the service. Processing is based on steps taken at your request before entering into a contract and, where applicable, on performing the contract and complying with legal obligations such as invoicing.

How long we keep it. We keep quote requests that do not go ahead for 12 months (to be confirmed). Client data is kept for the periods required by law.

Who we share it with. We do not sell or pass on your data. The form is sent through the FormSubmit service and messages are received by our email provider, both acting on our behalf. The website host may log technical data, such as IP addresses, for security reasons.

Your rights. You can ask to access, correct, erase, restrict or port your data, and object to its processing, by writing to geral@sustentabililand.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD) at www.cnpd.pt.

5. Cookies

This website does not use its own, advertising or analytics cookies. The Google Maps map is only loaded if you click "Show map", in which case Google may use cookies. Links to WhatsApp and Facebook open external services with their own policies.
