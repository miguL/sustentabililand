Frequently asked questions

Common questions

If you can't find your answer here, give us a call or send us a message.

Is the quote free?

Yes. Quotes are free and with no obligation. Tell us what you need and, if necessary, we'll arrange a visit to assess the space.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Loulé and work across the Algarve, including Almancil, Quarteira, Vilamoura, Faro, Olhão, Albufeira and Tavira.

Which type of air conditioning is right for me?

It depends on the space. A wall-mounted split is the simplest option for a single room. A multi-split cools and heats several rooms with a single outdoor unit. Ducted air conditioning is hidden in the false ceiling and distributes air throughout the house. We'll help you choose during the visit.

How often should the system be serviced?

We recommend servicing once a year, including filter cleaning and a full check of the unit. Holiday rentals and heavily used spaces may need it more often.

Do you work for companies and on public works?

Yes. We work for homeowners, holiday rentals, businesses and builders. We hold Public Works Contractor Licence no. 83886-PUB, class 2, for contracts up to €400,000.

Do you also install solar panels and heat pumps?

Yes. We install solar thermal and photovoltaic systems, heat pumps and domestic hot water systems, and can integrate them with your air conditioning.

Which brands do you work with?

We are a multi-brand installer. We mainly work with Samsung and Hisense, but we also install and service other brands, depending on the client's preference and budget.
